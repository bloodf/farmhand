`FARMHAND_BRIDGE_TARGETS` (helper: `BRIDGE_TARGETS`) lists `ip:port` Moonraker bridges on the farmhand host, such as Kobralink for a Kobra X. An exact match may use the host's own address; the Spoolman allowlist step is skipped for it.
